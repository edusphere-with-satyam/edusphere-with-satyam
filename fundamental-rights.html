Fundamental Rights

Part III of the Constitution of India — Articles 12 to 35

Introduction

Fundamental Rights are basic rights guaranteed by the Constitution of India. They protect individual liberty, equality and dignity and provide protection against arbitrary action by the State.

Fundamental Rights are contained in Part III of the Constitution, covering Articles 12 to 35.
