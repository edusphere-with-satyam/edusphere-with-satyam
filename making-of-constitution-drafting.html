✍️ Drafting of the Constitution — Overview

After the Constituent Assembly and its committees discussed various constitutional issues, their decisions had to be converted into a proper legal document. This work led to the preparation and detailed discussion of the Draft Constitution.

Key Idea: Committee Reports → Drafting → Debate → Amendments → Final Constitution

📘 B. N. Rau — Constitutional Adviser

B. N. Rau was the Constitutional Adviser to the Constituent Assembly.

He studied different constitutions and prepared an initial draft which helped the Drafting Committee in its work.

Remember: B. N. Rau → Constitutional Adviser

🏛️ Drafting Committee

29 August 1947

Formation

The Drafting Committee was appointed on 29 August 1947. It was responsible for preparing the Draft Constitution.

Chairman

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

Dr. B. R. Ambedkar was the Chairman of the Drafting Committee and played a major role in shaping and presenting the Draft Constitution.

📖 Three Readings of the Constitution

First Reading

General Discussion

The broad principles and provisions of the Draft Constitution were discussed.

Second Reading

Detailed Discussion

The provisions were examined clause by clause and amendments were considered.

Third Reading

Final Discussion

The final Draft Constitution was discussed before its adoption.
