Class 11 Economics — Overview

Class 11 Economics introduces students to the basic concepts of microeconomics, statistics for economics, economic activities, consumer behaviour, production, markets and important economic issues.

Class: XI
Subject: Economics
Resources: Notes | Examples | Important Questions | MCQs | Revision

Part A — Introductory Microeconomics

Unit 1 — Introduction to Microeconomics

Basic concepts of economics, scarcity, choice, opportunity cost, production possibility frontier and central economic problems.

Unit 2 — Consumer Behaviour and Demand

Utility, consumer equilibrium, demand, determinants of demand, law of demand and elasticity of demand.

Unit 3 — Producer Behaviour and Supply

Production, factors of production, cost, revenue, supply and producer equilibrium.

Unit 4 — Forms of Market and Price Determination

Different market structures, price determination and the interaction of demand and supply.

Unit 5 — Simple Applications of Demand and Supply

Applications of demand and supply concepts in different economic situations.

Part B — Statistics for Economics

Unit 1 — Introduction to Statistics

Meaning, importance and uses of statistics in economics.

Unit 2 — Collection, Organisation and Presentation of Data

Understanding data, sources of data, collection of data, organisation of data and presentation through tables and diagrams.
