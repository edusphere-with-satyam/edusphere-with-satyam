1. GDPMP = ₹1,500 crore, Depreciation = ₹100 crore, Indirect Tax = ₹200 crore, Subsidy = ₹50 crore and NFIA = ₹(–)50 crore. NNPFC is:
Answer: (a) ₹1,150 crore
NNPFC = GDPMP − Depreciation − Net Indirect Tax + NFIA.
NIT = 200 − 50 = 150.
= 1500 − 100 − 150 − 50 = ₹1,200 crore.
Therefore, the correct option should be (c) ₹1,200 crore.
2. Which of the following is NOT included in the domestic territory of India?
Answer: (d) Both (a) and (b)
Embassies and branches of Indian enterprises located abroad are treated as part of India's economic territory, while foreign embassies in India are excluded from India's domestic territory.
3. A foreigner stays in India for only 120 days and starts a business here. For the purpose of National Income accounting, he is generally treated as:
Answer: (b) Non-resident
A person is normally considered a normal resident when their centre of economic interest lies in the country and they have an economic interest for a sufficiently long period. A short stay of 120 days does not normally qualify.
4. Value of Output = ₹2,000 crore, Intermediate Consumption = ₹800 crore and Net Change in Stock = ₹(–)200 crore. Value Added is:
Answer: (b) ₹1,200 crore
Value Added = Value of Output − Intermediate Consumption.
= 2000 − 800 = ₹1,200 crore.
The change in stock is already considered while calculating Value of Output.
5. Which of the following will cause double counting?
Answer: (b)
Adding the value of intermediate goods along with the final product counts the same value more than once. This is called the problem of double counting.
6. Operating Surplus does NOT include:
Answer: (c) Mixed Income of Self-Employed
Operating Surplus consists mainly of rent, royalty, interest and profit. Mixed income is separately estimated for self-employed persons.
7. Which of the following income is included in National Income?
Answer: (c)
Commission earned by a dealer for providing a current productive service is included in National Income. Lottery winnings and pensions are transfer receipts, while the sale of an old car itself is not current production.
8. If GDP Deflator = 200% and Real GDP = ₹500 crore, Nominal GDP is:
Answer: (b) ₹1,000 crore
GDP Deflator = (Nominal GDP / Real GDP) × 100.
200 = (Nominal GDP / 500) × 100.
Nominal GDP = ₹1,000 crore.
9. NFIA is negative if:
Answer: (a)
NFIA = Factor Income Received from Abroad − Factor Income Paid to Abroad.
Therefore, NFIA becomes negative when factor income paid abroad is greater than factor income received from abroad.
10. Real GDP = ₹800 crore and Nominal GDP = ₹1,000 crore. GDP Deflator is:
Answer: (b) 125%
GDP Deflator = (Nominal GDP / Real GDP) × 100.
= (1000 / 800) × 100 = 125%.
11. Which of the following is a transfer income?
Answer: (b)
A scholarship is a transfer receipt because it is received without rendering a productive factor service in return.
12. The Value Added Method is based on which formula?
Answer: (b)
Gross Value Added = Value of Output − Intermediate Consumption.
Depreciation is deducted later to obtain Net Value Added.
13. In the Income Method, GDPMP is calculated as:
Answer: (a)
GDPMP = Compensation of Employees + Operating Surplus + Mixed Income of Self-Employed + Depreciation + Net Indirect Taxes.
14. Which of the following is NOT normally included as a component of Compensation of Employees?
Answer: (d)
Compensation of Employees includes wages and salaries in cash, wages in kind/perquisites and employers' social security contributions. Interest benefit on an employee loan is not treated as a standard component of COE in the CBSE National Income framework.
15. Which activity may increase GDP but may not necessarily increase economic welfare because of negative externalities?
Answer: (b)
Production of tobacco contributes to GDP through market transactions, but its harmful effects may reduce social welfare. This illustrates why GDP is not a perfect measure of economic welfare.
16. Under the Expenditure Method, investment includes:
Answer: (c)
Investment expenditure includes expenditure on newly produced capital goods, such as construction of new houses. Purchase of financial assets such as shares is a financial transaction, while second-hand goods were already counted when originally produced.
17. Net Change in Stock is negative when:
Answer: (b)
Net Change in Stock = Closing Stock − Opening Stock.
Therefore, it is negative when Opening Stock is greater than Closing Stock.
18. Calculate GDPMP from the following data: Rent = ₹200 crore, Wages = ₹800 crore, Interest = ₹150 crore, Profit = ₹300 crore, Mixed Income = ₹100 crore, Net Indirect Tax = ₹150 crore and Depreciation = ₹50 crore.
Answer: (c) ₹1,750 crore
Operating Surplus = Rent + Interest + Profit
= 200 + 150 + 300 = ₹650 crore.
GDPMP = Wages + Operating Surplus + Mixed Income + Depreciation + NIT
= 800 + 650 + 100 + 50 + 150
= ₹1,750 crore.

Therefore, the correct answer is actually (d) ₹1,750 crore.
19. If factor income to abroad is ₹100 crore and factor income from abroad is ₹150 crore, NFIA is:
Answer: (a) ₹50 crore
NFIA = Factor Income from Abroad − Factor Income to Abroad.
= 150 − 100 = ₹50 crore.
20. Which of the following is included in Domestic Income but excluded from National Income?
Answer: (c)
Domestic Income includes factor incomes generated within India's domestic territory, including factor income paid to non-residents. Such income is deducted while moving from Domestic Income to National Income through NFIA.
