🇮🇳 Preamble — Overview

The Preamble is the introductory statement of the Constitution of India. It expresses the basic philosophy, objectives and ideals of the Constitution and indicates the source of its authority.

Key Idea: The Preamble tells us the nature of the Indian State and the objectives that the Constitution seeks to achieve.

📜 The Preamble

WE, THE PEOPLE OF INDIA

having solemnly resolved to constitute India into a SOVEREIGN SOCIALIST SECULAR DEMOCRATIC REPUBLIC and to secure to all its citizens:

JUSTICE, social, economic and political;

LIBERTY of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship;

EQUALITY of status and of opportunity;

and to promote among them all FRATERNITY assuring the dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the Nation;

IN OUR CONSTITUENT ASSEMBLY this twenty-sixth day of November, 1949, do HEREBY ADOPT, ENACT AND GIVE TO OURSELVES THIS CONSTITUTION.

🔑 Key Words of the Preamble

Nature of State

Sovereign

India is internally supreme and is free to conduct its external affairs without external control.

Nature of State

Socialist

India aims to reduce economic inequalities and promote social and economic justice.

Nature of State

Secular

The State does not establish any official religion and respects freedom of religion.

Nature of State

Democratic

The government derives its authority from the people through representative institutions and elections.

Nature of State

Republic

The head of the State is elected, directly or indirectly, and does not hold office by hereditary succession.

Objectives

Justice

Justice in the Preamble includes social, economic and political justice.

Objectives

Liberty

Liberty of thought, expression, belief, faith and worship.

Objectives

Equality

Equality of status and opportunity for citizens.

Objectives

Fraternity

Promotes brotherhood, dignity of the individual and the unity and integrity of the Nation.

📌 42nd Constitutional Amendment, 1976

The Preamble was amended only once by the 42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976.

Three words were added:

Socialist + Secular + Integrity

The expression "unity of the Nation" was changed to "unity and integrity of the Nation."

⚖️ Is the Preamble a Part of the Constitution?

1960

Berubari Union Case

The Supreme Court initially held that the Preamble was not a part of the Constitution.

1973

Kesavananda Bharati Case

The Supreme Court held that the Preamble is a part of the Constitution and can help in interpreting constitutional provisions.

Important: The Preamble is part of the Constitution, but it is not an independent source of power or limitation on legislative authority.

✏️ Can the Preamble Be Amended?

Yes. The Preamble can be amended under Article 368, subject to the Basic Structure doctrine.

Only one amendment so far:
42nd Constitutional Amendment Act, 1976

⭐ Significance of the Preamble

Source of Authority

The words "We, the People of India" show that the Constitution derives its authority from the people.

Constitutional Philosophy

It reflects the fundamental ideals and objectives of the Indian constitutional system.

Interpretation

It helps courts understand the purpose and philosophy behind constitutional provisions.

Identity of the State

It describes India as a Sovereign, Socialist, Secular, Democratic Republic.
