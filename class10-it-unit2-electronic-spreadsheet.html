📘 Electronic Spreadsheet (Advanced) — Overview

Electronic Spreadsheet is an important part of Class 10 Information Technology. In this unit, students learn advanced features of LibreOffice Calc for analysing, automating, linking, sharing and reviewing spreadsheet data.

Students learn how spreadsheet tools can be used to perform advanced calculations, analyse different possibilities, automate repetitive tasks, connect information between sheets and documents, and review changes made to a spreadsheet.
