Introduction to Unit 4

A safe and healthy workplace is important for protecting workers, preventing accidents and maintaining an efficient working environment. Every employee should understand workplace hazards and follow appropriate safety procedures.

This unit introduces students to workplace health, safety and security, different types of workplace hazards, safety policies and procedures, and basic practices that can help prevent accidents and emergencies.

CBSE Unit Focus:
Understand workplace health, safety and security policies, identify different workplace hazards, understand risks and learn practices that help maintain a safe working environment.
CBSE 2026–27:
Unit 4 carries 10 marks and includes 15 theory hours and 22 practical hours.

Unit 4 Sessions
