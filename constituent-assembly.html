📜 What was the Constituent Assembly?

The Constituent Assembly was the body formed to draft and frame the Constitution of India. It brought together representatives from different parts of British India and the Princely States to discuss and decide the constitutional framework of independent India.

In simple words: The Constituent Assembly was the body that debated, drafted and finally adopted the Constitution of India.

🏛️ Formation of the Constituent Assembly

Cabinet Mission Plan, 1946

How was it formed?

The Constituent Assembly was formed under the recommendations of the Cabinet Mission Plan of 1946.

Its members were not directly elected by the people. Representatives from the British Indian Provinces were elected indirectly by the Provincial Legislative Assemblies through proportional representation by means of the single transferable vote.

Representatives of the Princely States were nominated by the rulers of those states.

👥 Composition of the Constituent Assembly

The original strength of the Constituent Assembly was 389 members.

292 representatives from British Indian Provinces
93 representatives from Princely States
4 representatives from Chief Commissioners' Provinces
After Partition: The strength of the Constituent Assembly was reduced from 389 to 299 members.

🗓️ First Meeting of the Constituent Assembly

9 December 1946

First Meeting

The first meeting of the Constituent Assembly was held on 9 December 1946.

Dr. Sachchidananda Sinha acted as the temporary Chairman of the Assembly.

11 December 1946

Election of the President

On 11 December 1946, Dr. Rajendra Prasad was elected as the permanent President of the Constituent Assembly.

H. C. Mookherjee was elected as its Vice-President.

🎯 Objectives Resolution

On 13 December 1946, Jawaharlal Nehru moved the Objectives Resolution in the Constituent Assembly.

The Resolution described the broad ideals and objectives that were intended to guide the making of the Constitution.

22 January 1947: The Objectives Resolution was adopted by the Constituent Assembly.

It later became an important foundation for the philosophy and ideals expressed in the Preamble of the Constitution.

🇮🇳 Constituent Assembly After Independence

Before Independence, the Constituent Assembly was created under the Cabinet Mission Plan. After India became independent on 15 August 1947, the Assembly became a sovereign body.

It performed two important functions. It acted as the body responsible for framing the Constitution and also functioned as the legislative body for the newly independent India.

Important: The Constituent Assembly was therefore not only involved in constitution-making but also performed legislative functions after Independence.

⚙️ How did the Constituent Assembly work?

Discussion and Debate

Members discussed important constitutional questions and expressed different views on the structure and principles of the new Republic.

Committee System

Different subjects were examined through committees, whose reports helped the Assembly in preparing the Constitution.

Article-by-Article Discussion

The Draft Constitution was discussed in detail and members proposed amendments before the provisions were finally accepted.

⭐ Why was the Constituent Assembly important?

The Constituent Assembly provided a forum where representatives could debate the political, social and economic needs of India and transform these ideas into a constitutional framework.

Through detailed discussions, committee work and amendments, the Assembly prepared a Constitution designed to establish the structure of government and protect important constitutional principles.

Remember: The Constitution was not simply written by one person or one committee. It was the result of extensive discussion, debate and deliberation in the Constituent Assembly.
