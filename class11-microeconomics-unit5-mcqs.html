1. Market equilibrium occurs when:

Correct Answer: C
Market equilibrium occurs when quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.

2. The price at which quantity demanded equals quantity supplied is called:

Correct Answer: B
Equilibrium price is the price at which quantity demanded equals quantity supplied.

3. Excess demand occurs when:

Correct Answer: A
Excess demand exists when quantity demanded is greater than quantity supplied.

4. Excess supply occurs when:

Correct Answer: B
Excess supply exists when quantity supplied is greater than quantity demanded.

5. Excess demand generally creates:

Correct Answer: B
When demand exceeds supply, buyers compete for the available quantity, creating upward pressure on price.

6. Excess supply generally creates:

Correct Answer: B
Excess supply puts downward pressure on the market price.

7. If demand increases while supply remains constant, equilibrium price generally:

Correct Answer: A
An increase in demand generally raises both equilibrium price and equilibrium quantity, when supply remains constant.

8. If supply increases while demand remains constant, equilibrium price generally:

Correct Answer: B
An increase in supply generally lowers equilibrium price and raises equilibrium quantity, when demand remains constant.

9. A price ceiling is:

Correct Answer: B
A price ceiling is a government-imposed maximum legal price.

10. A binding price ceiling is generally fixed:

Correct Answer: B
A price ceiling becomes binding when it is set below the equilibrium price.

11. A binding price ceiling generally creates:

Correct Answer: B
A binding price ceiling can create excess demand, resulting in a shortage.

12. A price floor is:

Correct Answer: B
A price floor is a government-imposed minimum legal price.

13. A binding price floor is generally fixed:

Correct Answer: B
A price floor becomes binding when it is set above the equilibrium price.

14. A binding price floor generally creates:

Correct Answer: B
A binding price floor creates excess supply, resulting in a surplus.

15. If Qd = 80 and Qs = 50, the market has:

Correct Answer: B
Excess demand = Qd − Qs = 80 − 50 = 30 units.

16. If Qs = 100 and Qd = 70, the excess supply is:

Correct Answer: A
Excess supply = Qs − Qd = 100 − 70 = 30 units.

17. If Qd = 60 and Qs = 60, the market is:

Correct Answer: C
When quantity demanded equals quantity supplied, the market is in equilibrium.

18. If the demand curve shifts to the right, it represents:

Correct Answer: B
A rightward shift of the demand curve represents an increase in demand.

19. If supply decreases while demand remains constant, equilibrium price generally:

Correct Answer: B
A decrease in supply generally raises equilibrium price and lowers equilibrium quantity.

20. Which condition represents equilibrium?

Correct Answer: C
The basic equilibrium condition is Qd = Qs.
