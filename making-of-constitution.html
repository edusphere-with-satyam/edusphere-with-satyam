🏛️ Making of the Constitution — Overview

The Constitution of India was created through a detailed process of discussion, debate and deliberation by the Constituent Assembly. The process included constitutional developments under British rule, the formation of the Constituent Assembly, the work of various committees, drafting of the Constitution and its final adoption.
