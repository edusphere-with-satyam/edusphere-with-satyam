📚 Sources of the Indian Constitution — Overview

The Constitution of India was not copied from any single constitution. The Constituent Assembly studied different constitutional systems and adapted useful features according to Indian requirements.

Important: The Government of India Act, 1935 was one of the most important sources for the structural and administrative provisions of the Constitution.

🇮🇳 Government of India Act, 1935

Major Source

Important Features

• Federal scheme
• Office of Governor
• Judiciary
• Public Service Commissions
• Emergency provisions
• Administrative details
