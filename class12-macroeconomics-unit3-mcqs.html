Q1. At the break-even level of income, which statement is correct?

Correct Answer: C. Saving is zero

⭐ Must Remember for Board Exam

MPC + MPS = 1

APC + APS = 1

k = 1 / MPS

k = 1 / (1 − MPC)

At Break-Even Income:
C = Y
S = 0
APC = 1

At Equilibrium Income:
AD = AS

Two-sector economy:
Y = C + I

Higher MPC → Higher Multiplier

Higher MPS → Lower Multiplier

AD < AS → Inventories rise

AD > AS → Inventories fall

Deficient Demand → AD is insufficient at full employment

Excess Demand → AD exceeds the full-employment level
