1. What is production?

Answer: B
Production is the process of transforming inputs into goods and services.

2. Which of the following is a factor of production?

Answer: D
Land, labour, capital and entrepreneurship are factors of production.

3. The reward for labour is:

Answer: B
The reward for labour is wages.

4. The reward for capital is:

Answer: C
The reward for capital is interest.

5. In the short run:

Answer: B
In the short run, at least one factor of production remains fixed.

6. In the long run:

Answer: A
In the long run, all factors of production can be varied.

7. A production function shows the relationship between:

Answer: B
A production function represents the technical relationship between inputs and output.

8. Total Product means:

Answer: C
TP is the total quantity of output produced.

9. Average Product is calculated as:

Answer: B
AP = TP ÷ Units of variable factor.

10. Marginal Product is:

Answer: C
MP measures the additional output resulting from an additional unit of the variable factor.

11. The formula for Marginal Product is:

Answer: B
MP = ΔTP ÷ ΔL.

12. When MP is positive, TP:

Answer: A
When MP is positive, each additional unit of the variable factor adds to TP.

13. TP is maximum when:

Answer: B
TP reaches its maximum when MP becomes zero.

14. When MP becomes negative, TP:

Answer: B
A negative MP means an additional unit of the variable factor reduces total output.

15. AP is maximum when:

Answer: A
AP reaches its maximum when MP becomes equal to AP.

16. The Law of Variable Proportions deals with:

Answer: B
The law studies changes in output when a variable factor is increased while other factors remain fixed.

17. A rational producer generally operates in:

Answer: B
Stage II is considered the rational stage because MP is positive but diminishing.

18. In Stage III of production, MP is:

Answer: C
In Stage III, MP is negative and TP falls.

19. Fixed cost is a cost that:

Answer: B
Fixed cost remains unchanged with changes in output in the short run.

20. Which is an example of fixed cost?

Answer: B
Factory rent is commonly treated as a fixed cost in the short run.

21. Variable cost:

Answer: A
Variable cost changes according to the level of output.

22. Total Cost is equal to:

Answer: B
TC = TFC + TVC.

23. Average Fixed Cost is:

Answer: A
AFC = TFC ÷ Q.

24. Average Variable Cost is:

Answer: B
AVC = TVC ÷ Q.

25. Average Cost is:

Answer: A
AC = TC ÷ Q.

26. Which formula is correct?

Answer: A
Average Cost is the sum of Average Fixed Cost and Average Variable Cost.

27. Marginal Cost is:

Answer: B
MC is the additional cost resulting from producing one additional unit.

28. The formula for Marginal Cost is:

Answer: C
MC = ΔTC ÷ ΔQ.

29. When MC is less than AC:

Answer: B
When MC is below AC, it pulls the average cost downward, so AC falls.

30. MC intersects AC at:

Answer: B
MC intersects AC at the minimum point of AC.
