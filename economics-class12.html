📘 Class 12 Economics — Overview

Class 12 Economics focuses on Macroeconomics and Indian Economic Development. It helps students understand national income, money, banking, employment, government budget, balance of payments and important aspects of the Indian economy.

Class: XII
Subject: Economics
Resources: Notes | Examples | Numericals | Important Questions | MCQs | Revision

📊 Part A — Introductory Macroeconomics

Unit 1

National Income and Related Aggregates

National income, domestic income, national product, domestic product, GDP, GNP, NDP, NNP, real and nominal GDP, depreciation and related concepts.

Unit 2

Money and Banking

Meaning and functions of money, money supply, commercial banks, credit creation, central bank and monetary policy.

Unit 3

Determination of Income and Employment

Aggregate demand, aggregate supply, consumption, saving, investment, multiplier, equilibrium income and employment.

Unit 4

Government Budget and the Economy

Government budget, receipts, expenditure, revenue deficit, fiscal deficit, primary deficit and objectives of government budget.

Unit 5

Balance of Payments

Balance of payments, current account, capital account, foreign exchange rate, exchange rate determination and related concepts.

🇮🇳 Part B — Indian Economic Development

Unit 1

Indian Economy on the Eve of Independence

State of Indian economy before independence, agriculture, industry, foreign trade, demographic conditions and occupational structure.

Unit 2

Indian Economy (1950–1990)

Economic planning, goals of planning, agriculture, industry and trade policies during the planning period.

Unit 3

Liberalisation, Privatisation and Globalisation

Economic reforms of 1991, liberalisation, privatisation, globalisation and their impact on the Indian economy.

Unit 4

Human Capital Formation

Meaning of human capital, sources of human capital formation, education, health and their importance for economic development.

Unit 5

Rural Development

Rural credit, agricultural marketing, diversification, organic farming and important issues related to rural development.

Unit 6

Employment

Employment, unemployment, formal and informal sectors, worker participation and important employment-related issues.

Unit 7

Environment and Sustainable Development

Environment, environmental problems, sustainable development, pollution and strategies for sustainable development.

Unit 8

Development Experience of India — A Comparison with Neighbours

Development experience of India and selected neighbouring countries, with comparison of important economic and social indicators.
