📘 DPSP — Overview

The Directive Principles of State Policy (DPSP) are constitutional guidelines given to the State for establishing social and economic justice and promoting the welfare of the people. They are contained in Part IV of the Constitution.

Part: Part IV
Articles: 36–51
Nature: Non-Justiciable
Main Objective: Establishment of a Welfare State

⚖️ Article 37 — Nature of DPSP

Not Enforceable by Courts:
The provisions contained in this Part shall not be enforceable by any court.

Fundamental in Governance:
The principles are nevertheless fundamental in the governance of the country, and it is the duty of the State to apply them while making laws.

⚖️ DPSP & Fundamental Rights

Fundamental Rights protect important individual freedoms, while DPSPs guide the State towards social and economic welfare. Both form an important part of the constitutional framework.
