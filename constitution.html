🇮🇳 Indian Constitution — Overview

The Constitution of India is the supreme law of the country. It provides the framework of government, defines the powers and functions of constitutional institutions, and guarantees important rights and principles for citizens.

Step 1: Understand the historical background and making of the Constitution.
Step 2: Learn the Preamble and important constitutional features.
Step 3: Study Fundamental Rights article-wise with important cases.
