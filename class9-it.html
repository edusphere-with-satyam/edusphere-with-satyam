Class 9 IT — Overview

Information Technology (IT) is a skill-based subject that develops students' understanding of computers, digital tools, documentation, spreadsheets, presentations and workplace-related IT skills.

CBSE Subject Code: 402
Class: IX
Session: 2026–27

Part A — Employability Skills

Unit 1 — Communication Skills–I

Understanding communication, methods of communication, verbal and non-verbal communication, active listening and effective communication.

Unit 2 — Self-Management Skills–I

Understanding self-management, self-awareness, goal setting, time management and maintaining a positive attitude.

Unit 3 — Basic ICT Skills–I

Introduction to Information and Communication Technology, basic computer operations, hardware, software and safe use of digital tools.

Unit 4 — Entrepreneurial Skills–I

Introduction to entrepreneurship, characteristics of an entrepreneur, entrepreneurial values and the importance of entrepreneurship.

Unit 5 — Green Skills–I

Understanding environment-friendly practices, sustainable development, conservation of resources and the importance of a green economy.

Part B — Subject-Specific Skills

Unit 1 — Introduction to IT–ITeS Industry

This unit introduces students to Information Technology and Information Technology enabled Services (ITeS).

  • Introduction to IT and ITeS
  • IT-BPM industry
  • BPO and BPM
  • Growth of IT industry in India
  • Applications of IT in different fields
  • IT in education
  • IT in banking
  • IT in healthcare
  • IT in business
  • IT in communication
  • IT in government services

Unit 2 — Data Entry & Keyboarding Skills

This unit develops students' keyboarding and data-entry skills.

  • Introduction to keyboard
  • Types of keys
  • Alphanumeric keys
  • Numeric keypad
  • Function keys
  • Control keys
  • Navigation keys
  • Home keys and guide keys
  • Touch typing
  • Typing ergonomics
  • Mouse operations
  • Typing speed and accuracy
  • Typing practice using typing software

Unit 3 — Digital Documentation

This unit introduces students to creating and managing digital documents using word-processing software.

  • Introduction to word processing
  • Creating documents
  • Opening documents
  • Saving documents
  • Editing documents
  • Formatting text
  • Find and Replace
  • Spelling and grammar tools
  • Creating and formatting tables
  • Inserting images and shapes
  • Headers and footers
  • Page numbers
  • Printing documents
  • Mail Merge

Unit 4 — Electronic Spreadsheet

This unit introduces students to spreadsheets and their use for organising, calculating and presenting data.

  • Introduction to spreadsheet
  • Rows and columns
  • Cells and cell ranges
  • Entering data
  • Formulas
  • Operators
  • SUM function
  • AVERAGE function
  • MAX function
  • MIN function
  • COUNT function
  • Formatting spreadsheets
  • Fill Handle
  • Relative reference
  • Absolute reference
  • Mixed reference
  • Creating charts

Unit 5 — Digital Presentation

This unit develops students' skills in creating effective digital presentations.

  • Introduction to digital presentation
  • Elements of a presentation
  • Creating presentations
  • Creating and managing slides
  • Slide layouts
  • Formatting slides
  • Inserting tables
  • Inserting images
  • Inserting shapes
  • Slide Show
  • Animation
  • Slide transitions
  • Slide Master
  • Principles of an effective presentation
