1. A market is a system through which:

Answer: B
A market is a system or arrangement through which buyers and sellers interact for the exchange of goods and services.

2. Perfect competition is characterised by:

Answer: C
A large number of buyers and sellers is an important feature of perfect competition.

3. Under perfect competition, the product is:

Answer: B
Firms under perfect competition sell a homogeneous product.

4. A perfectly competitive firm is a:

Answer: B
An individual competitive firm cannot influence the market price and therefore accepts the prevailing price.

5. Which is NOT a feature of perfect competition?

Answer: D
Product differentiation is not a feature of perfect competition because products are homogeneous.

6. An industry is:

Answer: B
An industry consists of firms producing the same or closely related goods and services.

7. Equilibrium price is determined where:

Answer: C
Equilibrium price is established where market demand equals market supply.

8. The individual firm under perfect competition accepts:

Answer: B
The competitive firm is a price taker and accepts the market-determined price.

9. Total Revenue is:

Answer: B
TR represents the total sales receipts obtained from selling output.

10. The formula for Total Revenue is:

Answer: A
Total Revenue = Price × Quantity sold.

11. Average Revenue is calculated as:

Answer: B
AR = TR ÷ Q.

12. Under perfect competition, AR is equal to:

Answer: B
Since AR = TR/Q and TR = P×Q, AR = P.

13. Marginal Revenue means:

Answer: C
MR is the change in total revenue due to a change in quantity sold.

14. The formula for Marginal Revenue is:

Answer: B
MR = ΔTR ÷ ΔQ.

15. Under perfect competition:

Answer: C
For a competitive firm, price remains constant, so AR = MR = Price.

16. Producer's equilibrium refers to the output level at which:

Answer: B
Producer's equilibrium is the output level at which the firm's profit is maximised.

17. The first-order condition for producer equilibrium is:

Answer: B
The marginal revenue-marginal cost approach gives MR = MC as the equilibrium condition.

18. At equilibrium, if MR is greater than MC, the firm should:

Answer: B
When MR > MC, an additional unit adds more to revenue than to cost, so increasing output can increase profit.

19. If MC is greater than MR, the firm should:

Answer: B
When MC > MR, producing an additional unit adds more to cost than revenue, so output should be reduced.

20. For producer equilibrium, MC should generally be:

Answer: B
For a maximum-profit equilibrium, MC should be rising at the point where MC equals MR.

21. Profit is equal to:

Answer: B
Profit = Total Revenue − Total Cost.

22. When TR is greater than TC, the firm earns:

Answer: B
If total revenue exceeds total cost, the firm earns profit.

23. Normal profit occurs when:

Answer: B
When economic total revenue equals total economic cost, the firm earns normal profit.

24. Break-even point occurs when:

Answer: A
At the break-even point, total revenue equals total cost.

25. At the break-even point, the firm earns:

Answer: B
At break-even, the firm covers its total economic cost and earns normal profit.

26. The shutdown point is associated with:

Answer: A
In the standard short-run model, the shutdown point occurs at the minimum point of AVC.

27. At the shutdown point, the firm is just able to cover:

Answer: B
At the shutdown point, total revenue is just sufficient to cover total variable cost.

28. The short-run supply curve of a competitive firm is the portion of its:

Answer: B
The standard short-run supply curve is the rising portion of the MC curve above the minimum AVC.

29. If price is below minimum AVC in the short run, the firm should generally:

Answer: B
If price falls below minimum AVC, the firm cannot cover its variable cost through production and may temporarily shut down.

30. Which statement is correct for a perfectly competitive firm?

Answer: B
Under perfect competition, the firm is a price taker and therefore AR = MR = Price.
