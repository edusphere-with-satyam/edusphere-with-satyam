Unit 1 – National Income

📝 MCQ Practice – Set 2

20 Important Multiple Choice Questions

1. If Gross National Product at Market Price (GNPMP) is ₹1,000 crore and Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA) is ₹(–)50 crore, then Gross Domestic Product at Market Price (GDPMP) will be:

Correct Answer: (b) ₹1,050 crore
Explanation: GDPMP = GNPMP − NFIA = 1000 − (−50) = ₹1,050 crore.

2. Which of the following is an example of an intermediate good?

Correct Answer: (c) Wheat purchased by a flour mill
Explanation: Wheat is completely used up in the production process during the year to make flour.

3. Net Domestic Product at Factor Cost (NDPFC) is also known as:

Correct Answer: (a) Domestic Income
Explanation: National Income is NNPFC, whereas Domestic Income is NDPFC.

4. Real GDP is considered a better indicator of economic growth than Nominal GDP because:

Correct Answer: (b) It eliminates the effect of price changes
Explanation: Real GDP is calculated at constant base-year prices, measuring physical output changes only.

5. Which of the following items is included while estimating National Income by Income Method?

Correct Answer: (b) Commission paid to a broker on sale of second-hand goods
Explanation: Commission is a payment for a productive service rendered in the current year.

6. Unforeseen obsolescence of fixed capital assets is a part of:

Correct Answer: (b) Capital loss
Explanation: Unexpected damage or obsolescence due to accidents or natural disasters is a capital loss, not routine depreciation.

7. Difference between Net National Product at Market Price (NNPMP) and Net National Product at Factor Cost (NNPFC) is equal to:

Correct Answer: (a) Net Indirect Taxes
Explanation: Market Price − Factor Cost = Indirect Taxes − Subsidies = Net Indirect Taxes.

8. If Nominal GDP is ₹600 crore and Price Index (GDP Deflator) is 120, the Real GDP will be:

Correct Answer: (a) ₹500 crore
Explanation: Real GDP = (Nominal GDP ÷ GDP Deflator) × 100 = (600 ÷ 120) × 100 = ₹500 crore.

9. Which of the following is NOT a component of Operating Surplus?

Correct Answer: (d) Compensation of Employees
Explanation: Operating Surplus includes Rent, Royalty, Interest and Profit. Compensation of Employees is a separate primary income.

10. The non-marketed productive activities are not included in GDP due to:

Correct Answer: (b) Non-availability of reliable valuation data
Explanation: Unpaid domestic services lack a clear market value or price signal.

11. An Indian citizen working in the UNO office located in New Delhi receives salary. This income will be a part of:

Correct Answer: (b) National Income of India
Explanation: The UNO office in Delhi is outside India's domestic territory, so the income is treated as factor income from abroad and included in National Income.

12. Double counting can be avoided by using which of the following methods?

Correct Answer: (d) Both (a) and (b)
Explanation: Both the Value Added Method and counting only final goods help eliminate double counting.

13. Which one of the following is a stock variable?

Correct Answer: (b) Distance between Delhi and Mumbai
Explanation: Stock variables are measured at a particular point in time. Distance is a stock concept.

14. Gross Fixed Capital Formation includes:

Correct Answer: (d) All of the above
Explanation: Gross Fixed Capital Formation includes business investment, government fixed investment and residential construction.

15. In the calculation of National Income, 'Subsidies' are:

Correct Answer: (b) Deducted from Indirect Taxes to calculate Net Indirect Taxes
Explanation: NIT = Indirect Taxes − Subsidies.

16. Negative Net Factor Income from Abroad (NFIA) implies that:

Correct Answer: (b) Factor income earned from abroad is less than factor income paid to abroad
Explanation: NFIA = Factor Income Received − Factor Income Paid. If NFIA is negative, payments abroad exceed receipts from abroad.

17. Which of the following is treated as a transfer income?

Correct Answer: (d) All of the above
Explanation: Transfer incomes are receipts without any corresponding productive flow of goods or services.

18. If Net Domestic Product at Factor Cost (NDPFC) is ₹2,500 crore, Net Indirect Taxes are ₹300 crore, and Depreciation is ₹200 crore, then GDPMP will be:

Correct Answer: (a) ₹3,000 crore
Explanation: GDPMP = NDPFC + Depreciation + NIT = 2500 + 200 + 300 = ₹3,000 crore.

19. Which of the following creates a positive externality?

Correct Answer: (b) Construction of a public park in a residential neighborhood
Explanation: Positive externalities provide uncompensated benefits to third parties.

20. Value of Output is calculated as:

Correct Answer: (a) Sales + Closing Stock − Opening Stock
Explanation: Value of Output = Sales + Change in Stock, where Change in Stock = Closing Stock − Opening Stock.
