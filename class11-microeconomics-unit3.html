📘 Determination of Income and Employment — Overview

This unit explains how the level of income and employment is determined in an economy. It helps us understand the relationship between Aggregate Demand, Aggregate Supply, consumption, saving, investment and the equilibrium level of income.

Unit: Determination of Income and Employment
Important Focus: Aggregate Demand | Consumption & Saving | Investment | Income Determination | Multiplier | Full Employment | Excess Demand | Deficient Demand

The unit also explains how an economy can face excess demand or deficient demand and how appropriate fiscal and monetary measures can be used to correct these situations.
