📋 Important Committees of the Constituent Assembly

The Constituent Assembly divided its work among several committees. These committees examined specific constitutional issues and submitted their recommendations to the Assembly. Their work helped in preparing and organising the provisions of the Constitution.

Exam Focus: Remember the committee name, its chairman and its main area of work.

🏛️ Major Committees

Most Important

Drafting Committee

Chairman: Dr. B. R. Ambedkar

The Drafting Committee was responsible for preparing the Draft Constitution on the basis of the decisions taken by the Constituent Assembly and the reports of various committees.

Exam Point: Dr. B. R. Ambedkar is known as the Chairman of the Drafting Committee.
Major Committee

Union Powers Committee

Chairman: Jawaharlal Nehru

This committee examined and made recommendations regarding the powers and functions of the Union government.

Major Committee

Union Constitution Committee

Chairman: Jawaharlal Nehru

It dealt with the broad constitutional structure of the Union and examined the organisation and powers of the central government.

Major Committee

Provincial Constitution Committee

Chairman: Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

This committee examined the constitutional structure and working of the provinces and helped in determining the framework of provincial governments.

Important

Advisory Committee on Fundamental Rights, Minorities and Tribal & Excluded Areas

Chairman: Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel

The committee dealt with important questions concerning Fundamental Rights, minorities and the administration of tribal and excluded areas.

Sub-Committee

Fundamental Rights Sub-Committee

Chairman: J. B. Kripalani

It examined and made recommendations regarding Fundamental Rights that were to be incorporated into the Constitution.

Sub-Committee

Minorities Sub-Committee

Chairman: H. C. Mookherjee

It examined issues relating to the rights and safeguards of minorities in the constitutional framework.

Important

Rules of Procedure Committee

Chairman: Dr. Rajendra Prasad

This committee dealt with the rules and procedures that governed the working of the Constituent Assembly.

Important

Steering Committee

Chairman: Dr. Rajendra Prasad

It helped in coordinating the work of the Assembly and arranging its business and proceedings.

Important

Finance and Staff Committee

Chairman: Dr. Rajendra Prasad

It dealt with financial matters and staff-related arrangements of the Constituent Assembly.

🌳 Committees Related to Tribal Areas

North-East Frontier (Assam) Tribal and Excluded Areas Committee

Chairman: G. S. Ghurye

The committee examined constitutional arrangements concerning tribal and excluded areas in Assam and the North-East.

Excluded and Partially Excluded Areas Committee

Chairman: A. V. Thakkar

It examined administrative and constitutional arrangements relating to excluded and partially excluded areas.

📌 Important Note

The committees did not independently write the entire Constitution. They examined specific subjects and submitted recommendations, which were then discussed and considered by the Constituent Assembly.
